# Review workflow guardrail

The public review workflow must be neutral.

Do not ask a customer to rate the business privately and then make the Google Review link available only to customers who give a high score. Do not discourage unhappy customers from posting public reviews.

A compliant implementation can offer both actions to every customer:

- Leave a Google Review
- Send Private Feedback

Private feedback can trigger an internal service-recovery alert, but it must not remove or hide the public-review option.
