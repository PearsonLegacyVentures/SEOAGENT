# Google Business Profile Audit

## Goal

Make the Business Profile accurate, complete and commercially useful without stuffing it with irrelevant categories or services.

## Inputs

- business context
- current GBP fields/screenshots/export where available
- current local competitors
- website service pages and booking URLs

## Audit

### Identity

Check:

- exact business name;
- address or service-area configuration;
- phone;
- website;
- booking link;
- hours and special hours.

### Categories

- Confirm one accurate primary category.
- Add secondary categories only when they genuinely describe the business.
- Compare competitors for useful evidence, not as an instruction to copy irrelevant categories.

### Services

For each genuine service:

- service name;
- concise description;
- price where accurate and useful;
- matching website landing page where available.

Do not add services solely for keywords.

### Service areas

Use only areas the business actually serves. Service areas are operational information, not a license to claim every nearby market.

### Media and conversion

Check:

- recent, relevant photos;
- logo/cover quality;
- appointment/booking link;
- products/offers only where they help a customer choose;
- Q&A accuracy.

## Output

Return three sections:

- FIX NOW — inaccuracies or high-impact omissions;
- IMPROVE — useful enhancements;
- LEAVE ALONE — fields already correct or risky changes without evidence.
