# Review Workflow

## Goal

Increase the number of genuine customer reviews while giving the business a fast private-feedback channel.

## Non-negotiable rule

Do not condition access to Google Reviews on a customer's rating or sentiment.

Every customer who receives the review request must have the same opportunity to leave a public Google review.

## Customer flow

1. A completed service triggers the request after an appropriate delay.
2. The message thanks the customer and asks for honest feedback.
3. Present both actions to every customer:
   - **Leave a Google Review**
   - **Send Private Feedback**
4. Google Review opens the official review URL.
5. Private Feedback opens a short form/message route.
6. Negative private feedback can alert management for service recovery.
7. Do not hide the Google option after negative private feedback.

## Suggested customer copy

Thank you for choosing {{business_name}}. We’d appreciate your honest feedback about your experience today.

**Leave a Google Review**
{{google_review_url}}

**Send Private Feedback**
{{private_feedback_url}}

## Internal feedback payload

Capture only what is useful for resolution:

- customer name (if provided);
- booking/reference ID when available;
- contact method;
- feedback;
- timestamp;
- resolution status.

## Reply policy

- Positive public review: reply personally and specifically where practical.
- Negative public review: do not auto-publish a generic defensive response; route for human review.
- Never fabricate reviews or employee/customer identities.
