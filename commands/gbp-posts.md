# GBP Posts

## Goal

Keep the Google Business Profile current and useful while sending users to the correct landing page.

## Default cadence

Prepare 8 posts for a four-week cycle unless the business has a better event/promotion schedule.

## Content mix

Rotate among:

- real service spotlight;
- booking/use case;
- location or visitor context;
- current offer only when genuinely active;
- proof/update/event;
- educational buyer question.

## Rules

- No invented discounts, availability, reviews or outcomes.
- Use one clear CTA.
- Link to the specific relevant page, not automatically to the homepage.
- Add UTM parameters to website links.
- Avoid duplicating the same copy across multiple posts.
- Use images that genuinely match the service or update.

## UTM convention

`utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp&utm_content=<short-post-id>`
